# Surface: index.html (single-page portfolio)

Scope: whole site, one scrolling page. Visitor mode: Experience (portfolio) with a Read register — the work and credentials lead, the interface recedes.

Audience and job: recruiters, internship coordinators, hackathon organizers verifying Kim De Guzman's credentials and projects in under two minutes, often on a phone from a resume or LinkedIn link. Action: contact via email/phone or open GitHub/LinkedIn.

Proof/content: real content from TASK.md only — education, five skill groups, three roles, three projects, five certifications, contact links. No images yet; placeholders at final asset paths.

Constraints: plain HTML/CSS/JS, zero build, Vercel static. Visual world pinned to the bryl-minimal-design skill (monochrome, Geist/Geist Mono/Geist Pixel, numbered section headers, mono micro-labels, halftone dots, soft shadows, fade-up entrance, light/dark/system theme). Only its language, none of bryllim.com's content or extra features.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet technical zine about one student's shipped work. Refuses the hero-metric template and the icon-card grid; hierarchy comes from font role switches and hairlines, not size or color.

OWN-WORLD: pure white / #0a0a0a ink (dark: #0c0c0f / #f4f4f5) with a nine-step gray ramp, no accent. Geist body 15px, Geist Mono 9–11px uppercase tracked labels, Geist Pixel Square lowercase display. Hairline gray-200 borders, 16/12/8/6 radius ladder, low-alpha ground-contact shadows, one halftone field dissolving out of the hero corner and inside every image placeholder. Inverted ink chip reserved for award badges.

STORY: visitor reads the name and role, scans numbered sections down a narrow column, recognizes real projects and awards, and leaves via a contact link believing Kim ships end to end.

FIRST VIEWPORT: fixed 14rem left sidebar nav (≥1024px) with numbered anchors and theme toggle; content column 42rem. Hero: mono label "ligao city, albay · philippines", pixel-font "kim de guzman" ~3rem, role line, two-sentence intro, then a mono link row (email, phone, GitHub ↗, LinkedIn ↗, Facebook ↗) and a halftone field fading from the top-right corner. Primary action: the email link.

FORM: the numbered-section long scroll of the pinned world; chosen by brief, no candidate list was generated. Seed: brief-pinned.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
