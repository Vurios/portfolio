01 — about

Hi, I'm Kim! I enjoy building software that solves problems I can see around me, and I want what I build to genuinely help people and make their day-to-day easier. The projects I care about most began with a real need close to home: a city routing emergencies through 20+ hotlines, a police station managing crime and incident reports by hand, and a library that wanted its catalog to answer questions directly. Working on them taught me to take ownership of the whole stack and to apply AI where it removes a genuine step for the person using the system.

Along the way I completed a TESDA scholarship program and interned with teams in Quezon City and Singapore. I also led a remote intern team and placed in hackathon and coding competitions across Albay. I work well under pressure and have remained on the President's and Dean's List throughout my degree. Outside of coursework, I keep building: refining the systems I've already shipped and looking for the next idea that can make a real, lasting difference in someone's community.
